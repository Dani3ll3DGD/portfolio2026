Hi, I'm
Danielle

My name is Dani, Im a Graphic and Web designer working in the Puget Sound region, I specialize in creating graphics that blend storytelling with my personal touches. I mostly focus on making content and visuals that make the ideas clear while also keeping my colorful bubble style. I usually get my ideas from social media so I can design things based on trends and the modern vibe. I aim to work with brands that need help pushing their identity, but my overall goal is to grow as a designer and build me own network of design.

I believe art should feel alive — full of energy, surprise, and a genuine point of view. Every piece is made with intention and a little bit of joy.

Commissions, prints, and collaborations are always welcome.
