About the Artist

Hi, I'm
Danielle

My name is Dani — a graphic and web designer working in the Puget Sound region. I specialize in creating graphics that blend storytelling with personal touches, making content and visuals that make ideas clear while keeping my colorful, distinctive style.

I draw inspiration from social media trends and modern aesthetics, designing with the contemporary moment in mind. My goal is to work with brands that need help building their identity, while growing as a designer and building my own network.

I believe art should feel alive — full of energy, surprise, and a genuine point of view. Every piece is made with intention and a little bit of joy.

Graphic Design Web Design Illustration Typography

The Work

Bold color, playful composition, and storytelling-through-design. Every project is approached with care for craft and a distinct visual identity.

The Process

Research, sketch, refine, repeat. I pull from current trends while anchoring every piece in strong typographic and compositional fundamentals.

Let's Collaborate

Available for commissions, brand identity projects, and creative collaborations. Based in Puget Sound, working with clients everywhere.
